First-time hiring checklist

Use it for any artisan: plumber, electrician, AC technician, tailor, mechanic and more. Tick as you go.

1Before you hire

  • Describe the job clearly and take 2–3 photos of the problem
  • Get 2–3 quotes so you know the fair price
  • Check verified badges, work photos and reviews
  • Ask if the price is workmanship only, and what parts cost
  • Agree the start date and how long the job will take

2Agree in writing

  • Put the full price and what it covers in the app chat
  • Agree how you'll pay: part at the start, the rest at the end
  • Ask who buys materials, and keep every receipt
  • Agree they'll fix problems from their own work
  • Share your exact address only with the person you hire

3During the job

  • Be there at the start, or have someone you trust there
  • Keep old parts until the job is finished
  • Ask before any extra work that changes the price
  • Keep valuables and documents out of the work area

4When it's done

  • Test everything: taps, switches, sockets, cooling, fit
  • Pay the balance only when you're satisfied
  • Mark the job complete in the app
  • Leave an honest review to help the next customer

!Stop and think if they…

  • Ask for the full payment before starting
  • Won't give a price until the work is underway
  • Won't show receipts for parts they buy
  • Ask you to pay outside what you agreed in the chat
  • Pressure you to decide immediately
  • Take shortcuts with safety (wiring, gas, generators indoors)
